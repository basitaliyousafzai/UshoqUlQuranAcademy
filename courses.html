Our Academic Courses

Carefully structured syllabi designed to take students from basic Arabic phonetics to complete fluency, memorization, and profound Quranic comprehension.

Level 1: Beginner Foundation

Noorani Qaida for Kids & Beginners

The quintessential stepping stone for non-Arabic speakers. We teach letter shapes, accurate throat and tongue articulation points (Makharij), and how individual Arabic letters join to form words.

  • Individual & compound Arabic alphabet mastery (Huroof Mufradat & Murakkabat)
  • Harakat (Fatha, Kasra, Damma) and Tanween (Double vowels)
  • Sukoon, Tashdeed (Shaddah), and Maddah (Prolongation rules)

Level 2: Recitation Rules

Applied Tajweed & Melodious Tarteel

Reciting the Quran without Tajweed alters word meanings. This course equips students with both theoretical comprehension and daily vocal application of Tajweed rules.

  • Rules of Noon Sakinah & Tanween (Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab, Ikhfa)
  • Rules of Meem Sakinah and heavy/light letter rules (Tafkheem & Tarqeeq)
  • Waqf (Stopping regulations) and breath control techniques
